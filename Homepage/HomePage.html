Welcome to 'Health & Wellbeing (Health & Wellbeing For Teenagers)'!
Year 12 and 13 are the most stressful, yet the most important time to work on your health and wellbeing, having good health and wellbeing will mean having sufficient focus in class, good general health, and will have positive effects on relationships. So wait no more! Dive into the different sections of this webpage and explore the different areas of health and wellbeing: nutrition, sleep hygiene, fitness, and mindfulness/mental health.
